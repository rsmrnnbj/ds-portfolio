A Few Things to Remember

You don't have to have everything figured out at the beginning of the semester. Some topics will make sense quickly, while others might take a little more time. Keep learning, keep your work organized, and give yourself enough time to make mistakes and fix them.

When something doesn't work, read the error first. It is easy to immediately look for an answer, but understanding what the error is trying to tell you can help you solve similar problems on your own later.

AI is part of learning now. It can be a useful assistant when you are stuck, confused about a concept, or need another way of explaining something. Use it to help you understand, not just to give you something to submit. Check the answers, ask follow-up questions, and make sure you understand the work you are putting your name on.

Don't be afraid to ask questions. Sometimes the thing you are confused about is also something other people don't understand but are too shy to ask.

And lastly, don't compare your progress too much with everyone else's. Some people understand things faster, some need more practice, and some things just take time. What matters is continuing to learn and giving yourself the chance to improve.

You don't need to know everything at once. Keep learning, stay curious, and give yourself enough time to figure things out.
